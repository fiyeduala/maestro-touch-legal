# Staff quick start

For lawyers, case officers, finance officers and content editors.

## Signing in

1. **Set your password.** Open the invitation link you were emailed. It works once and expires; ask an
   administrator for a new one if it has lapsed.
2. **Sign in** at `/admin` with your email and password, then the 6-digit code from your authenticator app
   (or the one emailed to you).
3. **Keep it private.** Never share your account. On a shared computer, sign out when you leave. Signed-in pages
   are not stored by the browser.

You only see what your role allows. Lawyers and case officers see only the matters they are on the team for.

## My work

**My work** is your start page. It shows your open tasks, deadlines coming up, and matters waiting on you.

## Lawyers and case officers

- **Enquiries.** For each new enquiry, record the conflict search. Only an administrator clears or flags a
  conflict.
- **Matters.**
  - Keep the stage and the next action current. The client sees those, not your internal notes.
  - **Internal notes** are for the team only.
  - **Client chat** is what the client reads. Do not give legal advice in chat before the engagement is approved.
- **Documents.**
  - Files uploaded here are confidential and never public.
  - A draft deliverable reaches the client only when it is approved and released.
- **Notarisation.**
  1. Record the client's consent first.
  2. Submit to Naija Virtual Notary yourself.
  3. Enter NVN's reference and update the status as it changes.

## Finance officers

- **Invoices.**
  - Issue invoices in NGN or USD. Amounts are never converted.
  - Correct an issued invoice with a credit note; it is never edited.
- **Payments.**
  - Card payments confirm themselves through Paystack.
  - Mark a bank transfer paid only when the money is in the bank statement. A client's screenshot is not proof.
  - A refund is complete only when it is confirmed.
- **Client funds.** The client-funds ledger is kept separately. Nothing is deducted from it or paid out
  automatically.

## Content editors

- **Pages** (Website → Pages): edit, then preview the draft, then publish. Earlier versions are kept.
- **Blog posts.** Write, choose categories and tags, add a cover image from the media library, then publish.
- **Comments.** Approve or reject them.
- **Media library.** Images and PDFs, up to 10 MB. **Everything uploaded here is public**, so never put client
  documents in the media library.

## If something looks wrong

Tell the technical administrator what you did, what you expected and what happened, with the time. Do not send
screenshots that show client details outside the firm.
