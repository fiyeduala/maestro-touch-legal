1. Who we are

Maestro Touch Legal ("we", "us") operates mtouchlegal.com and its client portal. For questions about your personal data, contact [privacy contact email].

2. What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, optional phone number, password (stored only as a one-way hash), and the dates you accepted our terms and this policy.
  • Enquiries and consultations: the information you give us about your legal matter, and any documents you upload.
  • Matter records: messages, documents, invoices, payments and notes created while we act for you.
  • Job applications: the details, CV and supporting documents you send through our "Join Our Legal Team" form.
  • Technical data: IP address, browser type and sign-in times, used to keep accounts secure and to keep an audit trail of important actions.
  • Payments: card payments are handled by Paystack; we receive the payment reference and status, not your full card details. For bank transfers we keep the proof of payment you upload.
  • Live chat: our public pages use the Tawk.to chat service, which processes the messages you send through it under its own privacy policy. It is not used inside the client portal.

3. Why we use it

To create and secure your account, respond to enquiries, provide legal services, bill and receive payment, meet our professional and legal obligations, assess job applications, and send you the notifications you need (for example, a daily email summary of new portal messages).
